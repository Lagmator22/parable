# Who uses Parable's public record

Parable is for bot builders who want a record that includes failed runs, and for people evaluating a trading agent before trusting its reported performance. A builder can share the register. A reader can inspect trades, outcomes and stop reasons without creating an account.

Try the [live Graveyard](https://parable-demo.centralindia.cloudapp.azure.com/#register) or [query Rabdi directly](../README.md#query-the-public-record). The Graveyard links stopped bots to their Arkiv entities and, when available, Sepolia transactions. Model comparisons include both running and stopped bots.

After Mumbai, the next product step is a shareable creator record that uses the same public evidence. The current deployment is a testnet demo with practice funds.
